# IT Governance Matrix

| Action | Requester | Reviewer | Approver | Materiality | Evidence | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Create account | IT after HR employee exists | Identity/IT | governed approver | NEEDS DECISION | employee reference + request | account/access provisioning |
| Add workspace access | employee/manager/IT per policy | workspace owner | authorized approver | NEEDS DECISION | business reason | approved access request |
| Assign privileged role | authorized identity operator | security/governance | designated approver | high / NEEDS DECISION | justification + audit | role assignment |
| Suspend account | authorized operator | security/HR as required | policy-defined approver | NEEDS DECISION | reason | account state |
| Revoke access | authorized operator | workspace owner/security | policy-defined approver | NEEDS DECISION | reason/evidence | access state |
| Revoke session | identity operator | security | policy-defined approver | NEEDS DECISION | incident/request | session event |
| Release change | IT change owner | technical reviewer | change approver | NEEDS DECISION | test/evidence | release record |

Final requester, reviewer, approver, materiality, and evidence rules are NEEDS DECISION / NEEDS CONTRACT. IT_ADMIN is not a universal business superuser.
