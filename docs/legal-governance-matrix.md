# Legal Governance Matrix

Status seluruh keputusan: **NEEDS DECISION / NEEDS CONTRACT / NEEDS BACKEND**.

| Action | Requester | Reviewer | Approver | Materiality | Evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Catat kontrak | authorized user | Legal | governance sesuai kebijakan | NEEDS DECISION | kontrak resmi | contract record |
| Ajukan review legal | pemilik kebutuhan | Legal | NEEDS DECISION | sesuai objek | dokumen pendukung | review request |
| Catat perizinan | authorized user | Legal | NEEDS DECISION | jenis izin | dokumen izin | permit record |
| Catat perkara/klaim | Legal | Legal | governance | nilai/risiko belum ditetapkan | dokumen perkara | case record |
| Catat kewajiban | Legal | Legal | governance | tenggat/materiality | bukti kewajiban | obligation record |
| Konfirmasi status final | authorized approver | Legal | approver resmi | NEEDS DECISION | evidence terverifikasi | governed state |
| Terima hasil ekstraksi | authorized reviewer | Legal | sesuai objek | NEEDS DECISION | dokumen sumber | draft/candidate, bukan state final |

Frontend tidak menetapkan requester, reviewer, approver, materiality, atau lifecycle final tanpa sumber resmi.
